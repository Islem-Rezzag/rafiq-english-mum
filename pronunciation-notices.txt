رفيق — مدرّب الأصوات التجريبي / Rafiq experimental sound coach, version 3.0.0

This free feature runs acoustic phoneme recognition locally in the browser.
The audio and results are not uploaded or stored persistently by Rafiq.
It evaluates only the initial consonant of the listed practice words. It is not
a validated pronunciation assessment, a calibrated score, or a whole-word grade.
Thresholds and Arabic coaching are authored for this application, not supplied
or endorsed by the model's authors. The model can misrecognize fluent speech.

Filterbank implementation:
phoneme-fbank.js is a JavaScript implementation informed by the Apache-2.0
standalone Kaldi/Lhotse feature extractor in Thomas Guettler's Phoneme Party:
https://github.com/guettli/pp/blob/main/wasm/kaldi-fbank/fbank-standalone.zig
Changes: JavaScript FFT/filterbank implementation; fixed 16 kHz / 80-band settings;
no Zig/WASM extractor is redistributed. Apache-2.0 license: vendor/APACHE-2.0.txt
Numeric equivalence to Python Lhotse has not been independently established.

Verification limitations:
Synthetic reference/contrast recordings are engineering checks, not evidence of
accuracy for Arabic-speaking learners. No human pronunciation accuracy rate or
iPhone 13 Pro performance guarantee is claimed. This version needs real-device
and consenting learner evaluation before broader phoneme diagnosis is appropriate.
